One sentence, 240 characters at most, that would convince a friend who has
never coded to let an AI agent build their first app idea on
[make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-ronigrv-a-day-of-one-line-pitches-12cd9bb3).

## The entry

> Tell me your app idea in plain words and I'll have an AI agent build it for you on make.fun — no coding, no cost to try, and the app it makes is yours to keep.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) writes the sentence above to
`/output/entry.txt`. The sealed gate checks the file is UTF-8 text of 1 to
240 characters, and the house judge then compares it two at a time against
other entries on persuasiveness, clarity, truth, warmth and craft.
